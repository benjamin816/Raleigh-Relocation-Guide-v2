Moving to Raleigh? A Closer Look at Friendship Village

What daily life looks like in western Apex

If you’re moving to the Triangle and want a newly built home, Friendship Village gives you a concrete place to start comparing. Beazer Homes’ single-family community sits in the Friendship area of western Apex. On our visit, we paired the model-home tour with a look at the nearby shopping and recreation, because the layout is only half the decision.

Start with an ordinary Tuesday. Map the grocery run, drive to work at the hour you’d actually leave and check how you’d get to the park. We stopped near Publix on the tour. Pleasant Park is another useful stop if you want to see the area’s public recreation options. If school assignment matters, check the specific address with Wake County Public School System.

A first-floor primary suite changes how the house lives

The home we walked put the primary suite downstairs, with a large closet and a double vanity. Upstairs, the loft and a separate bonus room gave us two ways to spread out. One could be a work area while the other becomes a place to watch a movie. Ask which spaces are finished and what’s included on the plan and lot you’re pricing.

In the kitchen, the sink sits under a window and leaves the island clear for prep or seating. We liked the storage, but the model didn’t have a walk-in pantry. The separate front dining room also gives you an alternative to putting a large table between the kitchen and living room.

Walk the lot before you pick the plan

The screened porch was the spot we wanted to linger. On the home we toured, it had room for a table and looked out toward trees, with backyard space beyond it. Walk the actual homesite and check the view, grade and usable yard. The model’s porch and backyard are a useful reference for what you want to compare.

Before committing, ask for a written breakdown of the home, lot premium, selected options and any current incentives. The figures discussed in our June tour reflected that visit. For a relocation trip, we’d put Friendship Village next to one established Apex neighborhood and drive both routes on the same day. That makes it easier to see which location and layout would work for your routine.
