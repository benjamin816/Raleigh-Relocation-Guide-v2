# Living in Raleigh chat: conversation review

These are browser-tested conversations from the local Test site on October 27, 2026. Each ended at the call choice or resource handoff; no test appointment was placed. The common opening was: “Thanks for checking out the website! Are you looking to buy or sell real estate in the Raleigh area?” Three responses below show a final wording edit made immediately after the browser run: the North Raleigh acknowledgement, the first-sale acknowledgement, and the investor's Florida acknowledgement. All other lines are the recorded replies.
