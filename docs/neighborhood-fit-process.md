# Neighborhood Fit Process

## Goal
Work one neighborhood at a time, starting with Raleigh, and do not edit the repo until the photo and copy are approved in chat.

## Workflow
1. Choose one neighborhood.
2. Find 3 real photo options for that neighborhood only.
3. Present the photos in chat without committing anything yet.
4. Wait for approval, replacement request, or a new round of 3 options.
5. After the image is locked, draft the written block in the approved email style.
6. Wait for approval on the written block.
7. Only then update the repo and move to the next neighborhood.

## Photo rules
- Use real photos only.
- Prefer exterior or aerial neighborhood photos.
- Do not use AI-generated images.
- Do not hotlink random image URLs in final repo assets.
- Do not download or commit an image until the user approves it.
- If the neighborhood is being shown as a “section” or “district,” make sure the photo actually matches that use.
- If a neighborhood is not a real neighborhood, replace it with a real neighborhood.

## Copy rules
- Keep the email block simple and readable.
- Use the approved structure:
  - Number
  - Bold neighborhood name
  - Image
  - Short paragraph 1: neighborhood overview
  - Short paragraph 2: typical home types + starting price
  - Short closing line: who it fits
- Use fifth-grade-ish reading level language.
- Use Redfin or current listing research for price starting points.
- Include whether homes are older, newer, or a mix.
- Keep the tone natural, not AI-ish, not fluffy, and not overly polished.

## Conversation rules
- I lead the sequence and choose the next neighborhood.
- The user approves images and then approves copy.
- Do not jump ahead to the next neighborhood until the current one is approved.
- Do not work on multiple neighborhoods at once.
