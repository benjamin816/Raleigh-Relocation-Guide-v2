Why are people moving to West Apex?

If you’re moving to Raleigh and want a brand-new home, West Apex is one of the first places we’d tell you to look. You’ve got grocery stores and restaurants close by, Pleasant Park for a Saturday outside, and a route into Research Triangle Park, where Cisco, Lenovo and IBM have offices. The Apex Friendship schools are nearby, too. Check the specific address with Wake County before you count on an assignment.

When buyers tell us they love Cary or central Apex but can’t find a new home they like, this is where we pull up the map and move a little farther west. You can get a new house and still be part of a town where you already have places to shop, eat and spend the weekend.

For a first visit, we’d put a West Apex new build and a resale home closer to central Apex on the same tour. Start from each address and run the same two or three errands. It’s a better comparison than trying to judge both places from a listing photo.

Why we love Friendship Village

Ricardo and I walked Beazer’s model, but what sold us on taking a closer look was what we saw outside. We saw homesites backing up to trees, and from the screened porch we could see why that matters. It’s the kind of spot where you’d actually want to sit with your coffee. If you like that setup, have us walk the specific lot with you. The view and yard can change from one homesite to the next.

Inside, we liked the attention to detail. The model had its primary suite downstairs and a separate bonus room upstairs, so there’s room to spread out. But we’d start with the neighborhood: drive to the grocery store, swing by Pleasant Park and try the work route at the time you’d actually be driving it.

At Pleasant Park, you can get a feel for the public fields, courts and playgrounds the Town of Apex describes. At the community, ask Beazer what comes with the lot and floor plan you’re looking at. The model’s separate dining room, big porch and extra rooms are useful examples of how the space can work, and its kitchen had cabinet storage but no walk-in pantry.

Is Friendship Village right for you?

If you want a brand-new home and like the idea of living in an established suburb with shopping, recreation and access to RTP, put Friendship Village on your list. Apex isn’t the right pick for everybody. If your daily drive takes you the other direction, try it before you decide. And if you’re comparing a new build with resale in Apex or Cary, look at the whole deal: the price for the actual lot and options, what the builder warranty covers and whether there’s a rate incentive you can use.
