KALAS FALLS: BIG NEW HOMES NEAR RALEIGH

Wake Forest New Construction

Kalas Falls offers something buyers do not always expect this close to Raleigh: new homes with substantial square footage, larger homesites and a quieter setting near Rolesville. The real story, though, is how builder incentives can change the monthly-payment comparison.

Space Is the Headline

The tour focused on buyers who want more than a typical new-construction lot and floor plan. Kalas Falls pairs large homes with open space, trails and homesites that can provide views of trees, grass or fields instead of another house immediately behind you.

That combination can be especially appealing for households comparing newer communities with resale homes in Cary, North Raleigh or other closer-in locations where the same budget may buy less square footage.

Two Very Different Floor Plans

The Fleetwood shown in the video is approximately 2,558 square feet with four bedrooms and three bathrooms. It delivers a large open living area, a bonus room, a front porch and upgraded details without requiring buyers to jump to the community's biggest plan.

The Norman stretches to approximately 3,262 square feet with five bedrooms and three bathrooms. That additional space can support larger households, guests, work-from-home needs or flexible rooms, but the higher starting price makes the financing comparison even more important.

Why the Interest Rate Matters So Much

At the time of filming, the builder was advertising a 4.99% fixed-rate incentive on qualifying purchases. Using the assumptions discussed in the video, the estimated payment difference was roughly $500 per month on the Fleetwood compared with a 6.5% rate. The larger Norman also produced a lower estimated payment with the incentive than the smaller home did at the higher market-rate example.

Those figures were examples—not promises. Builder incentives, prices, loan programs and buyer qualifications change frequently. The useful lesson is that buyers should compare the complete monthly payment, cash required and long-term loan terms rather than judging two homes only by list price.

Location Without Giving Up Everyday Needs

Kalas Falls is marketed with a Wake Forest address and sits near Rolesville Road. The tour highlighted access to Rolesville schools, shopping, parks and routes toward Raleigh. For the right routine, it can deliver a quieter setting while keeping daily conveniences within reach.

As with any outer-ring community, test the commute at the hours you will actually drive. A spacious home and attractive incentive only make sense when the location also works for jobs, school, family and the places you visit every week.

What Buyers Should Verify

  • Current incentives: Confirm the exact rate, lender requirements, closing timeline and eligible inventory.
  • Homesite details: Compare grading, drainage, tree lines, future phases and what may eventually be built nearby.
  • Total payment: Include taxes, insurance, HOA costs and any mortgage insurance—not only principal and interest.
  • Builder contract: Understand deposits, deadlines, inspections, warranties and representation before signing.

Bottom line: Kalas Falls deserves attention if your priorities are a large new home, more outdoor space and a payment structure that may compete with smaller resale options. Just verify today's numbers before building a decision around them.

At A Glance

Kalas Falls may fit buyers looking for:

  • New homes from roughly 2,500–3,500 square feet
  • Four- and five-bedroom floor plans
  • Trails, open space and larger homesites
  • Access to Rolesville and northeast Raleigh
